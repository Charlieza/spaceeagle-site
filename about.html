// About SpaceEagle

An African space company,
built for what comes next.

SpaceEagle delivers future-ready satellite connectivity and Earth-observation intelligence across Africa. Our mission is to empower communities, strengthen enterprises, and support governments through next-generation digital infrastructure.

Our vision

We imagine a connected Africa where every individual, business, and institution has access to broadband and decision-ready intelligence — no matter how remote. The same satellite that watches a forest also brings a clinic online. The same data layer that informs a minister also helps a farmer plan a season.

What we do

01 / CONNECT

Satellite broadband

For homes, enterprises, and rural communities — built to perform where terrestrial networks don't reach.

02 / OBSERVE

Geospatial intelligence

For agriculture, climate, urban planning, and supply-chain risk — decision-ready, sector-specific.

03 / SECURE

Sovereign EO systems

For ministries and national agencies — independent capability, national-scale infrastructure.

04 / ENABLE

Capacity development

Specialised training that grows the next generation of African space and EO professionals.

Leadership

SpaceEagle is supported by directors with deep experience in astronomy, satellite missions, Earth observation, data science, and development impact. Our leadership focus is on delivering meaningful, practical solutions to real-world challenges across the continent — with the rigour of a space agency and the velocity of a modern technology company.
