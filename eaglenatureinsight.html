The bigger picture, around your business

Choose your business activity and the places that matter to it. Draw a site boundary or upload your area, explore the satellite view, and bring environmental data together with your own records and field observations.

The assessment explains what each available indicator measures, what its value suggests for your business, and what to check or do next. Water, vegetation, land cover, climate hazards and biodiversity context become a usable starting point for decisions.

A practical route through TNFD’s LEAP

The Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) provides the LEAP approach for identifying and assessing nature-related issues. EagleNatureInsight makes each step easier to work through.

  1. L

    Locate your interface with nature

    Map your sites and their surrounding landscape. See where operations meet water systems, ecosystems and areas of biodiversity importance.

    Your starting point: a site map and environmental context.
  2. E

    Evaluate dependencies and impacts

    Connect your business activities with the natural systems they use and influence. Add your own water, land-use and operational evidence to make that connection more specific.

    Your starting point: indicators, explanations and evidence gaps.
  3. A

    Assess risks and opportunities

    Review what the measured conditions could mean for production, costs and continuity. Prioritise the issues that need a closer check or a management response.

    Your starting point: business-relevant flags and suggested actions.
  4. P

    Prepare to respond and report

    Turn findings into an action plan. Download a PDF with your assessment’s results, maps, interpretations and recommended next steps to discuss with your team and partners.

    Your starting point: a clear plan and a shareable report.
