What is EagleNatureInsight™?

EagleNatureInsight™ is a lightweight, cross-sector nature intelligence service built on SpaceEagle's geospatial stack. It helps small and medium enterprises understand how their operations depend on and impact nature — and produces practical recommendations they can act on today.

Key capabilities

  • Automated LEAP assessment — Locates your interface with nature, evaluates dependencies and impacts, assesses risks, and prepares an easy-to-share summary.
  • Earth-observation powered — Satellite data maps land cover, water stress, hazards, and biodiversity sensitivity around your sites.
  • SME-friendly inputs — Works with just a location and sector selection — no specialist environmental knowledge required.
  • Low-bandwidth ready — Optimised for mobile and intermittent connectivity, so it can be used in remote or under-served areas.

How it works

01 / Simple inputs

Enter your business location, select your sector, and optionally add multiple sites. The platform handles the rest.

02 / Satellite analysis

EagleNatureInsight™ combines Earth-observation, hazard, and contextual data to identify key dependencies, impacts, and nature-related risks.

03 / TNFD-ready outputs

Receive a clear, LEAP-aligned scorecard and recommended actions to share with buyers, financiers, or regulators.

Who is it for?

  • SMEs in agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, retail, and services.
  • Corporate supply chains needing consistent nature-related data from suppliers.
  • Financial institutions and development programmes supporting SMEs on nature and climate disclosure.
